Spectrum and Chromatogram
==========================

.. automodule:: pymzml.spec


Data representation and integration
-----------------------------------

Imported data carries an explicit representation label, accessible through
the ``representation`` property:

- ``"profile"``: a densely sampled curve (profile spectrum or any
  chromatogram),
- ``"centroid"``: a sparse list of discrete centroid peaks,
- ``"reprofiled"``: a sampled curve generated by pymzml from centroid
  peaks, i.e. a converted result rather than imported data,
- ``"unknown"``: a user-created object without assigned data.

The two representations require different integration conventions, which
``integrate()`` selects automatically:

- Centroid peaks hold one intensity per peak, so their integral is the
  plain sum of peak intensities.
- Profile and reprofiled curves are integrated with the trapezoidal rule,
  i.e. the area under the sampled curve. The area is independent of the
  sampling density: re-sampling the same curve with more points does not
  multiply the integral, unlike a point-wise intensity sum. Consecutive
  samples are connected by linear interpolation. Passing
  ``gap_threshold`` additionally skips trapezoids that span unmeasured
  gaps instead of interpolating across them.

The integral computed by pymzml is independent of the TIC cvParam stored
in the mzML, which is reported by the instrument software and may follow
its own convention.
